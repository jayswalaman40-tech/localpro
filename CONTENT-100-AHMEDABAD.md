**How to use:** har idea ka ek **hook** (pehle 3 sec) + **kya dikhana hai** + **audience** diya hai.
Rule: reel <30 sec, **Gujarati/Hindi mix**, subtitles hamesha, CTA end me
("Helpzo app download karo" / "Free list karo — WhatsApp karo").
Cadence: **4–5 reels/week**. Areas rotate karo: Satellite, Bodakdev, Vastrapur,
Navrangpura, Maninagar, Bopal, Chandkheda, SG Highway, Prahladnagar, Paldi, Gota, Thaltej.
