# Helpzo — Market research, positioning & content strategy

_Prepared Sep 2026. Sources at the bottom._

## TL;DR — Chalega ya nahi?
**Haan, market real aur bada hai — lekin bheed bhi hai.** India ka home-services
market ~**$60 billion (FY2025)**, ~**10–11% CAGR** se **~$100B (FY2030)** ja raha
hai, aur **90%+ abhi bhi unorganized** hai — matlab jagah khaali hai. Tier-2/3
cities me smartphone penetration ise metros ke bahar khol raha hai.

**Lekin** Urban Company, Justdial, Sulekha pehle se hain, aur tumhare exact model
(direct-call, no-commission, WhatsApp) wale naye players bhi aa gaye hain (Jugaad,
DutiBook, CallSevai, PinkBazaar). To Helpzo **"kuch naya"** nahi hai — jeet
**execution + ek jagah pe focus (hyperlocal density)** se aayegi, na ki idea se.

**Verdict:** Worth building — **agar** tum ek chhoti jagah (jaise Ahmedabad ke
2–3 areas) me **dono taraf density** bana lo (kaafi providers + kaafi customers ek
hi ilaake me). Poore India ko ek saath target karoge to fail. **Ek mohalla jeeto,
phir agla.**

## 1. Market — data kya kehta hai
- **Size:** ₹5,100–5,210 bn (~$60B) FY2025 → ~$100B FY2030, 10–11% CAGR.
- **Unorganized:** 90.5% (FY2025) — bahut kam log kisi app se book karte hain.
- **Growth driver:** Tier-2/3 me smartphone + busy lifestyle.
- **Supply pool:** India me ~**250 million blue-collar workers**; ye log aksar
  **first-time internet users**, tech-savvy nahi → **simple app + WhatsApp +
  local bhasha** zaroori (Helpzo ke paas 5 bhasha hain ✅).

**Iska matlab Helpzo ke liye:** demand aur supply dono maujood hain; problem
"market hai ya nahi" nahi — problem "**trust + density + habit**" banane ki hai.

## 2. Competition aur khaali jagah (gap)

| Player | Model | Kamzori (yahi tumhara mauka) |
|---|---|---|
| **Urban Company** | Managed, premium, fixed price, ~20–30% commission | Mehnga; metros; providers ko commission chubhta hai; chhote/independent worker fit nahi hote |
| **Justdial** | Directory + pay-per-lead | Spammy, 10 providers ko ek lead, ghatiya experience, providers ka bharosa nahi |
| **Sulekha** | Lead 4–5 providers ko | Spam calls/SMS complaints; providers passive listing ke paise nahi dena chahte |
| **Naye (Jugaad, CallSevai, PinkBazaar…)** | Direct-call/WhatsApp, no commission | Chhote, local, brand weak — tumhare jaise; race execution ki hai |

**Khaali jagah (Helpzo ka wedge):**
1. **Independent worker-first** (jo Urban Company me fit nahi hote) — free listing.
2. **Direct call, no commission, no middleman** — worker ka pura paisa uska.
3. **Vernacular + simple** — tier-2/3 aur kam-padhe workers ke liye.
4. **Trust layer** — KYC verified badge + real reviews (Justdial/Sulekha yahi
   fail karte hain).

## 3. Helpzo positioning

> **"Aapke mohalle ke bharosemand kaarigar — seedha call karo, koi commission
> nahi."**
> _(Your neighbourhood's trusted workers — call directly, zero commission.)_

**Positioning pillars (har jagah yahi 4 baatein):**
1. **Seedha sampark** — customer ↔ worker, beech me koi nahi.
2. **Zero commission** — worker ko pura paisa (Urban Company se ulta).
3. **Verified & rated** — KYC + asli reviews (Justdial se ulta = no spam).
4. **Apni bhasha, apna ilaaka** — hyperlocal + 5 languages.

**Kya AVOID karo:** "India ka sabse bada services app" mat bolo. Bolo
**"Ahmedabad ka #1 local helper app"** — chhota, believable, jeetne layak.

## 4. Go-to-market — chicken-and-egg kaise todein
Marketplace ki asli problem: customer tab aayega jab providers honge; provider tab
tikega jab leads aayenge. Solution — **supply pehle, ek ilaake me:**

1. **Ek area chuno** (jaise Satellite/Bodakdev, Ahmedabad).
2. **50–100 providers** manually onboard karo (field pe jaake, WhatsApp worker
   groups me, local dukaan/labour naka pe). Free listing = easy yes.
3. Un providers ko bolo apne **existing customers** ko Helpzo se call karne ko
   kaho (worker khud demand laayega).
4. **Customer demand** us ilaake me content + pamphlet + QR se banao.
5. Ek area me kaam kar jaye (leads flow ho) → **case study banao** → agla area.

> Density = jeet. 1 city me 500 active providers + regular customers, 20 cities me
> 20-20 se behtar hai.

## 5. Content strategy (posts + reels) — DO alag audience

India me **413M Instagram users** (duniya me sabse zyada). Reels **3–5x/week**
static se **2x engagement** dete hain. Rule: **<30 sec, Hindi-English/Gujarati
mix, subtitles hamesha, pehle 3 second me hook.**

### Channels (mix)
- **Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts** — reach/branding
- **WhatsApp Status + Broadcast** — providers aur warm customers ke liye (tier-2 me sabse strong)
- **Local Facebook groups** ("Ahmedabad Buy/Sell", area groups) — demand
- **Offline** — pamphlet + QR code chowk/apartment/kirana pe (tier-2 me abhi bhi kaam karta hai)

---

### A) PROVIDER-targeted content (SUPPLY banane ke liye)
**Goal:** worker soche "mujhe free me kaam milega, isme naam likhwana chahiye."

**Message angles:**
- "**Ghar baithe kaam paao — bilkul free, koi commission nahi.**"
- "**Justdial ke paise band karo** — Helpzo pe listing free hai."
- "2 minute me apna kaam list karo, customer seedha call karega."

**Reel ideas (provider):**
1. **Success story** — "Rajesh bhai (plumber) ko is hafte **18 naye customer** mile — Helpzo se, 0 commission." (asli worker, asli awaaz)
2. **How-to** (screen recording) — "Aise banao apni free listing — 2 minute me." (app dikhaao)
3. **Comparison** — "Urban Company: 25% cut. Justdial: paise lo, spam do. Helpzo: **free + seedha call**."
4. **Trust** — "Verified badge kaise milta hai (KYC)" — jyada leads = verified.
5. **Day-in-life** — ek electrician ka din, Helpzo se aaye kaam ke saath.

**Hooks (pehle 3 sec):** _"Plumber ho? Ye 30 second tumhara mahina badal sakta hai."_ · _"Commission dena band karo."_

**CTA:** "Aaj hi free list karo — link bio me / WhatsApp karo."
**Cadence:** week me 2–3 provider reels.

---

### B) CUSTOMER-targeted content (DEMAND banane ke liye)
**Goal:** customer soche "koi kaam ho to Helpzo kholo" (habit).

**Message angles:**
- "**Ghar ka koi bhi kaam? 1 call door.**"
- "Aadhi raat nal toota? 2 minute me nazdeeki plumber."
- "Justdial ki 10 calls nahi — **ek bharosemand banda**."

**Reel ideas (customer):**
1. **Problem → solution (7 sec)** — nal se paani behta hua → app pe search → call → "ho gaya." (fast, satisfying)
2. **Before/after transformation** — painter/deep-cleaning ka before-after (ye sabse zyada viral hote hain).
3. **Speed demo** — screen record: search "electrician" → 3 verified log → call. "Itna aasaan."
4. **Local flex** — "Satellite ke top 5 verified electrician — Helpzo pe."
5. **Trust/review** — asli customer testimonial: "verified aaya, time pe aaya, sahi daam."
6. **Festival/season hooks** — Diwali cleaning, monsoon me waterproofing/AC service.

**Hooks:** _"Ye kaam ke liye kabhi jyada paise mat dena."_ · _"Ahmedabad me plumber dhoondhna ab 10 second ka kaam."_

**CTA:** "Helpzo app download karo — apne ilaake ke verified log."
**Cadence:** week me 2–3 customer reels + 1 testimonial.

### Production tips (sasta + effective)
- Phone se shoot, **subtitles zaroori** (log bina awaaz dekhte hain).
- **Asli log** dikhaao (worker/customer) — polished ad se zyada trust.
- Har reel me **ilaaka + bhasha** local rakho (Gujarati/Hindi).
- Trending audio use karo, lekin pehle 3 sec me hook.
- Ek hi reel ko Instagram + YouTube Shorts + WhatsApp status — teeno pe daalo.

## 6. Kaunse numbers track karo
- **Supply:** active providers per area, listing completion, KYC verified %.
- **Demand:** app installs, searches, **calls/leads per day**, repeat customers.
- **Health:** provider response rate, review rating, leads-per-provider (agar ye
  gir raha hai to demand kam hai).
- **North star:** **weekly completed leads per active provider** (isse dono side ka
  health pata chalta hai).

## 7. Risks + pehle kya validate karo
- **Chicken-egg** — sabse bada risk. Pehle 1 area me supply+demand density.
- **Trust/safety** — fake listing/ghatiya worker se brand marता hai → KYC strict.
- **Monetization baad me** — abhi free se users banao; jab providers ko regular
  leads milne lagein, tab subscription (₹) ya pay-per-lead — providers tabhi
  denge jab value dikhe (Sulekha yahi pe fail hui: passive listing ke paise koi
  nahi deta).
- **Pehla test:** 1 area, 50 providers, 4 hafte content + field. Dekho **din ke
  kitne real calls** aate hain. 4 hafte me organic calls badhein = signal green.
